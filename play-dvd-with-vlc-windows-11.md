# How to Play a DVD with VLC Media Player on Windows 11

**What you need:** VLC Media Player installed, a DVD movie, and a computer with a DVD drive (built-in or connected by USB).

1. Click the **Start** button (the Windows logo) on the taskbar at the bottom of the screen.
2. Type **VLC media player**.
3. Click **VLC media player** in the search results to open it.
4. Insert the DVD into the DVD drive and close the tray.
5. In VLC, click **Media** in the upper-left corner.
6. Click **Open Disc**.
7. Under **Disc Selection**, make sure **DVD** is selected.
8. Click **Play**.
9. If the DVD's menu appears, click **Play** or **Play Movie** on that menu.

**Expected result:** The DVD movie plays in VLC Media Player.
