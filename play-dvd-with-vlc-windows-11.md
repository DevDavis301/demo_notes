# How to Play a DVD with VLC Media Player on Windows 11

**What you need:** VLC Media Player installed, a DVD movie, and a computer with a DVD drive (built-in or plugged in by USB).

1. Insert the DVD into the DVD drive and close the tray.
2. Press the **Windows** key on your keyboard.
3. Type **VLC media player**, then press **Enter** to open it.
4. Hold down **Ctrl** and press **D** to open the disc window.
5. Under **Disc Selection**, make sure **DVD** is selected.
6. Click **Play**.
7. If a DVD menu appears, click **Play** or **Play Movie** on that menu.

**Expected result:** The DVD movie plays in VLC Media Player.
