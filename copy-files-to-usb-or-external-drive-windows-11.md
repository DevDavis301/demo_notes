# How to Copy Files to a USB Flash Drive or External Drive on Windows 11

**What you need:** A Windows 11 computer and a USB flash drive or external drive already set up for Windows, with enough free space for your files.

**Before you begin:** Save and close any files you plan to copy. Copying puts a second copy on the drive and leaves the original files on your computer.

1. Click **Start** (the Windows logo) on the taskbar at the bottom of the screen.
2. Type **File Explorer**, then click **File Explorer** in the search results to open it.
3. In File Explorer, click **This PC** on the left.
4. Connect the drive to a matching USB port on the computer, using the drive's USB cable when needed. Connect its power supply if it has one.
5. Wait for the drive to appear under **Devices and drives**. Remember its name and letter, such as **USB Drive (E:)**.
6. On the left, click the folder containing your files, such as **Documents**, **Pictures**, or **Downloads**. Double-click any folders inside it until you see the files you want to copy.
7. At the top of File Explorer, click **View**, then **Show**. Click **Item check boxes** if it is not already checked. Click an empty area of the window to close the menu.
8. Move the mouse pointer over each file or folder you want to copy and click the small check box that appears. Check only the items you want to copy.
9. At the top of File Explorer, click **Copy** (the two overlapping pages icon).
10. Click **This PC** on the left, then double-click the drive you identified in step 5.
11. At the top of File Explorer, click **Paste** (the clipboard icon).
12. Leave the drive connected until copying finishes. Check that the files now appear on the drive.
13. Repeat steps 6–12 for files in other folders.
14. Close File Explorer by clicking the **X** in its upper-right corner.
15. Near the clock at the bottom-right of the screen, right-click **Safely Remove Hardware and Eject Media** (the small USB icon). If it is hidden, first click the small upward arrow near the clock.
16. Click **Eject** followed by your drive's name.
17. Wait for Windows to say it is safe to remove the drive, then unplug it.

**Expected result:** Copies of your selected files are stored on the USB flash drive or external drive. The original files remain on your computer.
